02 / life

Life, roughly:

Most days are a mix of working, coding, and playing games while listening to music all the time. I work around software, testing code, and building cool projects that help me carry out a niece thing in a quick and efficient manner.

04 / notes

Some notes:

Sorry Firefox/Safari users, interpolate-size is still in beta. Sorry :(
001 Software gets worse when it tries to own the user

A lot of apps feel less like tools and more like systems trying to trap people into their way of doing everything.

002 Simple pages are underrated

Not every thought needs a full post. Some things are better as a line, a link, or a rough note left somewhere visible.

003 Bad defaults ruin good software

The fastest way to make something feel hostile is to choose defaults that help the company more than the person using it.

004 Spacing matters more than people admit

Bad spacing makes a page feel fake immediately. Too even, too boxed-in, too balanced, and suddenly it looks generated.
